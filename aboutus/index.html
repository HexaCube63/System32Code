About Team System32Code

Built by students of OES Patan to spread practical cyber awareness in the age of Generative AI.


Our Mission & Vision


🏫 Who We Are

We are Team System32Code, a group of student developers from OES Patan studying about web development and digital safety.

🎯 Why This Project

As AI tools and online threats evolve rapidly, students and everyday users need clear, simple guides to recognize scams, deepfakes, and weak security habits.

🛡️ What We Built

An interactive awareness hub covering core cyber hygiene, modern AI training risks, and actionable self-check audits—coded from scratch in HTML & CSS.
